The three, in one sequence

The slash wordmark sits on the left (B). A quiet star waits in the space to its right, with twinkling stars across the whole screen (E). As the name slides in, the star ignites and the outburst expands into the open space (C). The sequence plays once. Then everything stays on screen: the stars keep twinkling, the companion keeps orbiting, and the name keeps shimmering until a key is pressed.

What shrinks, and what doesn't

The side-by-side layout needs 96 columns. Below that the same pieces drop out in a fixed order, so a narrow terminal still gets something that reads.

What building it involves
  • Spec: docs/chunks/C21-splash-sequence.md. Build it after C20.
  • Only tui/splash and the splash plumbing in tui/ui change. Render gains an elapsed-time argument.
  • The model already has a clock tick (tickMsg through Clock.After). The splash asks for a tick every 66 ms while it is on screen, and stops when a key or the first event dismisses it.
  • The generated cell data goes away. The 97 × 49 mark in mark_gen.go and its downscaling are replaced by two small letter bitmaps and a few shaders.
  • Tests use the fake clock. Each stage is checked at a fixed time, and the held frame is a golden file.

The README banner can come from the same bitmap in a later step.

Decided

  1. Order. Name and ignition overlap. The "outburst first" toggle above is only here for comparison and is not part of the spec.
  2. After the sequence. Nothing fades. The shell stays as a faint ring, the spikes stay at a third of their peak, and the name keeps shimmering. Pick "Once, then hold" and wait 8 seconds to see it.
  3. Replays. The same sequence plays on every launch, then holds. There is no short version.
